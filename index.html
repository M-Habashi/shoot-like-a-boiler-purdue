Calculation methods
  • Source. The official competition files: ESPN play-by-play, schedule and team files for NCAA women's basketball, from the SportsDataverse wehoop package. Season , the only season in the files with a floor location on every shot: field-goal attempts in games with play-by-play. Totals cover the games in these files, which may not be every NCAA game.
  • Shot filter. One shot is one field-goal attempt (FGA), made or missed. Free throws are excluded. Removed: attempts credited to a team not in the game, two-pointers located more than 24 feet from the hoop, three-pointers located less than 21 feet from it, and attempts by teams outside the -team list. Kept: FGA, of them by Purdue in games.
  • Distance and shot type. Distance d = √((x − 25)² + y²) feet, where x runs 0 to 50 across the floor and y is feet from the hoop along the court. At the rim: two-point attempts with d under feet. In between: all other two-point attempts (mid-range and floaters). Threes: three-point attempts as the source labels them. Layups: two-point attempts with play type LayUpShot (dunks and tip shots are separate types; LayUpShot scored as a three is excluded).
  • Points per shot. PPS = (2 × made twos + 3 × made threes) ÷ FGA, which equals 2 × eFG%. Every rate is a ratio of season totals (total points ÷ total FGA), not an average of game or team rates. The shot chart groups shots by whole-foot floor location and shows spots with at least FGA; brightness is PPS at that spot.
  • Other teams. The comparison pools the other teams: all-team totals minus Purdue's, per shot type and per floor spot ( FGA). Other teams PPS; Purdue , or more.
  • Ranks. Rank = 1 + the number of teams with a strictly higher value, computed from exact counts, so ties share the better rank. Top share = rank ÷ , rounded up to a whole percent. Purdue: at the rim, on threes, in between, on all shots. Layup FG%: Purdue , ; other teams . Big Ten ( teams): at the rim, on threes, on all shots. "Typical team" is the median team.
  • Margin of error. Standard error of Purdue's PPS = standard deviation of points per attempt (0, 2 or 3) ÷ √FGA. Two standard errors: ± at the rim, ± on threes, ± on all shots. This is a sensitivity range for luck in Purdue's own shots, not a confidence interval for the rank.
